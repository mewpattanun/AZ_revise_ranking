## Positive-control impact (v2.1 → v3 after P6 complete)

| Query | Control | v2.1 rank | After P6 |
|---|---|---|---|
| ERBB2 high | SKBR3 (HER2) | #30 | **#16** |
| ERBB2 high | BT474 (HER2) | #32 | **#20** |
| EGFR high | A431 | #19 | **#5** |
| EGFR high | Total hits | 2/3 | **3/3** |
| ESR1 high | Total hits | 1/3 | **3/3** |
| ERBB2+ESR1 | BT474 | #9 | **#6** |

Cells without protein data were previously capped at `w_rna = 0.95`,
pushing EGFR-amplified A431 to rank #19. After uncapping, biology drives
rank and A431 moves to top 5.

## Trade-offs (by design)

Full-coverage cells drop a few ranks as un-capped RNA-only cells now
compete fairly (e.g., HCC1954 #2→#9). This is intentional — the cap was
previously hiding single-modality strong signals.
